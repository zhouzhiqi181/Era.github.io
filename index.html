About EXO

EXO is a South Korean-Chinese boy band formed by SM Entertainment in 2011 and debuted in 2012. The group consists of nine members: Xiumin, Suho, Lay, Baekhyun, Chen, Chanyeol, D.O., Kai, and Sehun.

EXO's name comes from the word "exoplanet," and their concept incorporates superpowers and space themes. They are one of the most successful K-pop groups, having sold over 100 million records worldwide.
